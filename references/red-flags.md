# Red-flag checklist

Check every item and report findings in plain language. These are prompts for review, not legal conclusions.

## Title chain
- A gap between holders with no document.
- A deceased owner without both a death certificate and a family surviving / legal heir certificate.
- Heirs missing from a partition or release (including married daughters and minors).
- Partition decree not registered, or its registration number not matching the EC.
- Schedule dimensions differing between grant, decree, khata and agreement.
- Authority grant with an unexpired non-alienation clause or use restriction.

## Encumbrance
- EC periods with gaps.
- Mortgage or deposit entries without a matching registered release.
- Borrower is a firm or third party, not the owner; closure must cover every facility secured by the property.
- Loan classified as NPA: the closure amount keeps growing, and SARFAESI action is possible.
- Letters of continuity extending a deposit to new limits.

## Khata and tax
- Khata in a name other than the seller.
- Tax arrears or penalty; e-khata issued only recently.
- Built-up area on khata versus a valuer treating buildings as scrap.

## Agreement of sale
- e-stamp issued after the stated execution date.
- Stamp article and duty: an agreement that hands over possession is often chargeable like a conveyance in Karnataka, not at a nominal agreement rate.
- Name or relationship errors ("C/o" vs "S/o"), wrong address.
- More than one agreement for the same property with different prices.
- Possession handed over before title is cleared.
- Payments to the seller before the seller's loan is closed.

## Paying off the seller's loan
- Written in-principle sanction from the buyer's bank first.
- Exact closure figure in writing for the payment date.
- Pay directly to the loan account, never via the seller.
- Signed receipt or addendum tying the payment to the sale consideration.
- Collect no-dues for all facilities, original deeds, and registered releases; then a fresh EC.
