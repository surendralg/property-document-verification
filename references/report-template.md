# Title report template

Keep the user's existing format if a report exists. For a new report:

1. **Title**: "Property Title Report - <portion> of <site>, <layout>, <city>"
2. **Summary**: two short paragraphs on what is being bought, who owns it and on what basis, and whether the chain is unbroken. Then a table: Seller, Purchaser, Nature of property, Basis of seller's title, Agreement of sale.
3. **Property schedule**: the schedule text, then a Field / As per records table (address, property ID, khata, dimensions, site area, built-up area, property tax, valuation, Sub-Registrar), then a boundaries table comparing decree and khata.
4. **Ownership history**: one sentence on how many holders and how title passed, an optional chain diagram, then a table: Date | Event | Holder(s) after the event | Source document. The last row is the agreement of sale or proposed sale.
5. **Encumbrances**: one sentence on EC coverage, then EC period | Issued | Entries found, then a short paragraph explaining any bank entries and what must happen before the sale deed.
6. **Documents**: "The N supporting documents follow this report in date order, each page labeled, with an index of documents and page numbers."
7. Footer line: "Prepared by <name> from the copies of the documents enclosed. This summary is not a legal opinion."

Writing rules: dates as DD-MM-YYYY, rupees with Indian grouping ("Rs. 4,50,00,000"), names exactly as on the documents, and every claim tied to a source document.
