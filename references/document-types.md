# Common Indian property documents

Grouped by the categories used in the document set. Karnataka names first, with equivalents where they differ.

## Grant (original title)
- **Allotment letter**: development authority (CITB, MUDA, BDA, KIADB) offers a site at a rate; check site number, layout, extent, price.
- **Possession certificate**: confirms full payment and handover; gives dimensions and boundaries.
- **Title deed / sale deed by authority**: registered conveyance from the authority. Check for conditions (industrial use, non-alienation period) and the schedule.

## Civil records
- **Death certificate**: Registrar of Births and Deaths; note registration number and date.
- **Family surviving certificate / legal heir certificate**: issued by the Tahsildar or Revenue Inspector. Lists heirs with relationship, age and marital status. Watch for heirs who predeceased.

## Court decrees
- **Final decree for partition**: civil court decree, often on a compromise petition or through Lok Adalat. Engrossed on stamp paper and, when it creates title to immovable property, registered at the Sub-Registrar. The registered decree serves as the partition deed and carries a document number such as XXX-1-12345-2010-11.
- **Sketch / plan annexed to decree**: part of the decree; boundaries and measurements must match the schedule.
- **Release**: one co-owner gives up a share, often for cash recorded in the decree.

## Khata and tax
- **Khata endorsement / khata certificate**: municipal record of the owner for tax. Not title, but banks require it.
- **e-Khata (Form 2 / Form 3)**: Karnataka's digital khata with a property ID; shows site area, built-up area, year of construction, and the documents relied on.
- **SAS returns / tax paid receipts**: self-assessment property tax. Look for arrears paid late with penalty.
- **Application for khata transfer**: owner's letter to the municipality citing the decree or deed.

## Encumbrance
- **EC Form 15**: lists registered transactions for a period. **Form 16 (Nil EC)**: no transactions. Kaveri online ECs carry an application number and digital signatures.
- EC periods should cover the whole history. Old periods come from manual registers; recent ones from Kaveri.

## Legal opinions
- **Legal opinion / legal scrutiny report**: advocate's review for a bank. Lists documents seen, original or copy, and a final certificate on marketable title. Useful to confirm which documents existed at the time.

## Bank
- **MODTD (memorandum of deposit of title deeds)**: records an equitable mortgage by deposit of deeds. Registered in Karnataka; the EC shows it as a deposit of title deeds (DTD).
- **Letter of continuity**: confirms that an earlier deposit continues to secure a new or enhanced limit. The EC may also show it as DTD.
- **Credit sanction letter**: names the borrower (often a firm), limits, collateral and guarantors.
- **Loan closure / tentative closure letter**: amount to close, NPA status, conditions.
- **Valuation report**: government approved valuer; guideline rate, land value, building condition.

## Sale
- **Agreement of sale**: on e-stamp paper. Check the certificate number, issue date and time versus execution date, stamp article and amount, parties, consideration split, possession clause, and timelines for khata and loan clearance.
- **Sale deed**: final registered conveyance.

## Registration number format (Karnataka)
`<SRO code>-<book>-<doc number>-<financial year>`, for example `MYN-1-01234-2015-16` (Mysuru North, Book 1, document 1234, FY 2015-16). The SRO code changes when jurisdiction moves (Mysuru North vs East), so a request quoting the wrong prefix usually means the same document. CD numbers identify the scanned volume.
